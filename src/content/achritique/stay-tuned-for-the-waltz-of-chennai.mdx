Some projects are planned. Others are inevitable. The Waltz of Chennai belongs to the
second kind. It was born from years of travel between Houston and Chennai, a deep love for
ballroom dance, and the Tamil film songs that have soundtracked both cities of my life.

The film is a short one, but it carries a long journey inside it. Anchored by the timeless
rhythm of the Viennese Waltz, it traverses seven iconic Tamil film songs, originally
composed by the legendary AR&nbsp;Rahman and Anirudh&nbsp;Ravichander, each reimagined
across a different soundscape: orchestral symphony, jazz, rock, lo‑fi, Carnatic, and an
explosive dappangkuthu finale.

<Pull>Violin, dance and movement, blended into one breathtaking artistic journey.</Pull>

None of it would exist without the team. Director Shara&nbsp;Shanth and the dedicated crew
at 37Factor guided this project across eighteen months of artistry and perseverance:
through arrangements and re-arrangements, rehearsals and re-takes, and the small decisions
that turn an idea into a film.

In seven days, we bring something beautiful to the world. A visual symphony is almost
here.

Stay tuned.
